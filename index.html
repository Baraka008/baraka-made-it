About me

I design experiences with purpose.

I enjoy creating websites that are easy to use, visually appealing, and built around the user’s needs. I approach every project by thinking about the audience first, then translating that understanding into a layout, structure, and interaction that feels natural and professional.

One of the best examples of my work is the Liuwa Gardens project, a hotel and hospitality website that includes room and service information, a food catalog, and a cart interaction. It demonstrates both design thinking and practical front-end functionality.

Featured project

Liuwa Gardens

This project is a hospitality-focused website designed for a guest destination. It includes a welcoming landing page, service information, room highlights, food offerings, and an interactive cart system for ordering dishes.

The site reflects a strong front-end concept: elegant layout, warm colors, and a friendly user experience. It is also highly practical because it includes real functionality such as list rendering, adding items, and cart management with local storage.

  • Informative homepage for a hotel and garden setting
  • Food menu cards with add-to-cart interactions
  • Responsive navigation and modern layout styling
  • JavaScript-driven data updates and persistence
