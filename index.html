About me

I create practical, engaging, and user-focused web experiences.

My portfolio reflects an approach centered on clarity, function, and visual appeal. I enjoy turning ideas into digital products that feel clean, easy to use, and aligned with the purpose of the project.

Across my repositories, you will find a mix of static websites, front-end experiments, content-based designs, and more interactive builds. Some projects focus on hospitality and services, while others explore UI structure, digital layouts, and app-like experiences.

I like building things that not only look good but also work smoothly for the user. That is why many of my projects include organized layouts, responsive styling, dynamic content, and front-end logic that adds real value.

Featured project

Liuwa Gardens

Liuwa Gardens is a hospitality website concept designed around a natural and welcoming hotel experience. It introduces the guest to the destination, explains the services offered, highlights the rooms, and presents food options in a styled menu layout.

The project shows how front-end design can be both aesthetic and practical. It includes navigation, responsive layout adjustments, customized styling, and JavaScript functions for adding food items to a cart and storing them in local storage.

  • Hotel and hospitality website concept
  • Menu and food card system
  • Interactive cart behavior
  • Responsive layout and styling
